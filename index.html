About us

Welcome to WALMACK OÜ

Your trusted partner for end-to-end solutions in global trade. As specialists in export and import operations, our group of companies is committed to simplifying the complexities of international commerce.

We work with importers in target countries, helping them navigate the overwhelming selection of products and materials, verify suppliers, manage compliance, and facilitate export-import procedures without leaving their country.

Our core competencies

  • Identifying reliable suppliers across Europe and Asia
  • Skilled negotiation of sale and delivery terms
  • Secure financial transactions and payment assurance
  • Supply chain optimization and transport management
  • Customs support and trade barrier resolution
